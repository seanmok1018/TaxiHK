放鬆練習 Relaxation Exercises

Every breath is a fresh start. Click any exercise to see instructions.

• 找一個舒適的坐姿，放鬆肩膊
Find a comfortable seated position, relax your shoulders

• 用鼻子慢慢吸氣，數4秒
Inhale slowly through your nose for 4 counts

• 屏住呼吸，數4秒
Hold your breath for 4 counts

• 用嘴巴慢慢呼氣，數4秒
Exhale slowly through your mouth for 4 counts

• 重複4-6次
Repeat 4-6 times

❤️ 有助快速減壓，適合在等客或乘車途中進行。 / Great for quick stress relief.

• 从腳趾開始，緊繃肌肉5秒
Start with your toes — tense the muscles for 5 seconds

• 然後放鬆，感受緊張消退
Then release and notice the tension melting away

• 向上移至小腿、大腿
Move up to calves, then thighs

• 繼續至腹部、雙手、肩膊
Continue to abdomen, hands, and shoulders

• 最後到頸部和臉部
Finish with your neck and face

❤️ 幫助釋放駕駛或久坐後身體積累的肌肉緊張。 / Releases muscle tension from long sitting.

• 平躺或坐直，閉上眼睛
Lie down or sit upright, close your eyes

• 將注意力帶到呼吸上，放慢呼吸
Bring attention to your breath, slow it down

• 從頭頂開始，緩慢掃描全身
Starting from the crown of your head, slowly scan down

• 注意每個身體部位的感覺，不評判
Notice sensations in each area without judgment

• 如思緒飄走，溫和地帶回注意力
If your mind wanders, gently bring attention back

❤️ 提升對身體的覺察，有助舒緩長期慢性壓力與心理繃緊。 / Increases body awareness.

Name 1 thing you can taste

❤️ 快速將混亂的思緒帶回當下，非常適合在極度焦慮或恐慌時使用。 / Quickly anchors you.

Close your eyes and take three deep breaths

Notice the colours, sounds, and smells of that place

Allow yourself to be fully there for 5-10 minutes

Slowly open your eyes, carrying that calm with you

日常心理健康小貼士 / Tips

傾訴感受 / Talk About It

向信任的朋友、家人或輔導員表達情緒，不獨自承受壓力。

保持活躍 / Stay Active

每天步行30分鐘有助改善情緒，釋放內啡肽。

規律睡眠 / Regular Sleep

建立固定睡眠時間，充足睡眠對心理健康非常重要。

均衡飲食 / Eat Well

避免過多咖啡因及糖分，多攝取蔬菜水果維持平衡。
